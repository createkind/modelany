About ModelAny

An AI API aggregation platform, built by vincemakes.

Who is behind it

ModelAny is built by vincemakes, an independent studio that also builds AI image, video and productivity tools. ModelAny grew out of our own need: every product we ship talks to several model providers, and each one brings its own keys, limits and invoices.

The name

"ModelAny" means calling any model through one API. The official site is modelany.com. Other products with a similar name, such as browser extensions or chat tools, are not related to us.

Where things stand

ModelAny is in active design and development. We are not taking sign-ups or payments yet, and we do not collect email addresses on this site.
